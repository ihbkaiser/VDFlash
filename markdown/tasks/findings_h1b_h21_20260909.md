# Findings: H1b and H2.1 execution

This file records discoveries for the 2026-09-09 execution, separate from the
older smoke/debug plan.

- The current `run_msd` runner supports VDC only, prompt variants, retention
  conditions and model-parallel execution. It does not currently load MVBench.
- The cached model pair is Qwen2-VL-7B + MSD-Qwen2VL-7B; Qwen2.5-VL-3B is not
  cached, so new Qwen2.5 DFlash benchmark reruns remain unavailable.
- R2 on one VDC sample now completes for Full/Reduced/Deleted × real/zero
  visual values, with both `last_instruction` and `all_text` queries. The
  value hook changes the draft logits (`max |delta|=0.0234375`) and preserves
  top-1 on this sample. The raw probability KL is numerically 0 at this
  sample's very peaked distribution even in float64; this is recorded as an
  observation, not treated as evidence that the value hook is inactive.
- The draft logit scope is explicitly `draft_initial_next_token`; target-model
  `orig` logits are not used for the value-ablation KL.

## Full-run verification and results

- H1b VDC50 artifacts: `attention_kl_vdc50_part1_0_45.jsonl` (540 rows),
  `attention_kl_vdc50_part2_45_47.jsonl` (24 rows), and
  `attention_kl_vdc50_part3_47_50.jsonl` (36 rows). They cover 50 unique
  samples, 12 rows per sample, and contain no error rows.
- H1b MVBench artifacts: ten 100-sample chunks from
  `attention_kl_mvbench_000_100.jsonl` through
  `attention_kl_mvbench_900_1000.jsonl`. They cover all five selected tasks,
  1,000 unique samples, 12 rows per sample, and contain no error rows.
- H2.1 artifacts: `msd_natural_vdc50.jsonl` and
  `msd_answer_hint_vdc50.jsonl`, each with 150 rows (50 samples × Full,
  25% retention, Deleted), paired by sample and condition with no errors.

For the `last_instruction` query policy and real visual values, mean visual/text
attention mass is:

| Dataset | Condition | Visual mass | Text mass |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| VDC50 | Full | 0.8727 | 0.0953 |
| VDC50 | Reduced 25% | 0.6777 | 0.2394 |
| VDC50 | Deleted | 0.0000 | 0.6925 |
| MVBench | Full | 0.3313 | 0.4305 |
| MVBench | Reduced 25% | 0.1746 | 0.5117 |
| MVBench | Deleted | 0.0000 | 0.6390 |

The paired text-mass change is +0.1441 (95% bootstrap CI [0.1181, 0.1729])
from Full to Reduced and +0.5972 [0.5553, 0.6367] from Full to Deleted on
VDC50. On MVBench the corresponding changes are +0.0811 [0.0796, 0.0827] and
+0.2084 [0.2061, 0.2107]. The secondary concentration diagnostic also rises:
the mean top-five text-token mass on VDC50 changes from 0.0517 to 0.3256
(Full to Deleted), and on MVBench from 0.2127 to 0.3009.

The real-versus-zero value control gives exactly zero attention-mass difference
in all paired rows, as expected because the value intervention does not alter
Q/K. It nevertheless changes the captured first draft next-token logits: the
mean maximum absolute logit delta is 1.1622 (Full) and 0.5830 (Reduced) on
VDC50, and 0.2176 (Full) and 0.2199 (Reduced) on MVBench; top-1 match is
100% in these rows. Forward and reverse probability KL are 0 for all rows,
including float64 recomputation. The current KL therefore cannot discriminate
the distributions under the greedy, highly peaked output; max logit delta and
top-1 match are the usable diagnostics from this run.

For H2.1, mean accepted-prefix tokens are:

| Prompt | Full | Reduced 25% | Deleted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Natural | 1.1600 | 1.7471 | 2.6986 |
| Answer-hint | 1.1898 | 1.6345 | 2.4509 |

The Deleted–Full gain is +1.5386 for Natural and +1.2611 for Answer-hint. The
factorial interaction `(Hint Deleted−Full) − (Natural Deleted−Full)` is
−0.2776, with bootstrap 95% CI [−0.4094, −0.1411]. This does not support the
prediction that adding reference information removes the deletion effect.
However, Answer-hint changes the target prompt and therefore the target
continuation itself; all 150 Natural/Answer-hint target hashes differ. The
answer-hint run is consequently an oracle diagnostic of alignment, not a clean
accuracy test. No answer-accuracy metric was collected in this H2.1 runner.

## Interpretation and unresolved tests

- The attention-dilution mechanism is supported at the attention-allocation
  level: visual retention reduces visual mass and increases text mass in both
  datasets. This does not by itself prove that answers become less accurate.
- The over-attention prediction is partially supported by increased text
  concentration after deletion, especially on VDC50, but the proposed
  out-of-distribution hidden-state claim is not tested. The required training
  mean hidden-state distribution is unavailable in the workspace, so that
  comparison remains pending.
- The value ablation is causally active at the draft-logit input but not at
  captured attention, which is the expected separation between value and Q/K
  pathways. A non-saturated KL follow-up should use stored logits or rerun a
  small cohort with explicit logit-temperature calibration / margin metrics.
